The People Behind It

Meet the Founders

Real estate veterans turned technology innovators, building the future of property marketing in India and beyond.

Manik Aggarwal

Founder & CEO

“What if you could walk through a property before it even exists?” That question stayed with me long before this venture began, and honestly, it still drives every decision I make today. Coming from a technology-first mindset, I’ve always believed that the problem in real estate was never the lack of projects—it was the lack of real experience.... Buyers scroll endlessly through static images, cinematic videos, and brochures that all look perfect, but none of them truly answer the one thing that matters: how does it feel to be there? That gap is exactly where we decided to build. For me, this platform is not just about innovation for the sake of it—it’s about giving power back to the end user. I want a buyer sitting miles away to actually feel the space, understand proportions, light, movement, and even emotional connection with a property before making a decision. Technology today is capable of so much more than what the industry has been using, and it felt like the right time to bridge that gap. We’re building something that replaces imagination with experience—where you don’t have to “picture” your future home, you can step into it digitally. Every feature we design, every interaction we refine, is centered around simplicity and realism, because if it’s not intuitive, it’s not useful. I see this as a shift similar to how streaming replaced DVDs—once people experience it, there’s no going back. And that’s the core belief I bring into this: real estate should not be sold through visuals anymore, it should be experienced. We’re not just creating a product; we’re quietly reshaping expectations of how people Explore and connect with spaces. Read More

Puneet Goyal

Founder & CEO

Real estate doesn’t have a visibility problem—it has a conversion problem.” That’s something I’ve seen closely while working with developers and channel partners over the years. Projects are marketed aggressively, inventories are showcased everywhere, but somewhere between interest and decision, the connection drops... Developers struggle to communicate the true value of what they’ve built, and CPs often rely on repetitive site visits and explanations that don’t always translate into confidence for the buyer. That’s where our platform steps in—not just as a tool, but as a business enabler. From my perspective, this isn’t just about showcasing properties better; it’s about helping developers sell smarter and faster. When a project can be experienced instead of explained, conversations become clearer, trust builds quicker, and decisions happen more naturally. For channel partners, this changes the game completely—they’re no longer limited by location or availability; they can present projects in a way that feels premium, consistent, and impactful every single time. What excites me the most is how this aligns everyone in the ecosystem. Developers get a stronger narrative for their projects, CPs get a powerful sales edge, and buyers get clarity without friction. It’s rare to find a solution that benefits all sides equally, and that’s exactly what we’re building towards. I see this platform becoming a standard, not an alternative—because once businesses realize that immersive experience leads to better engagement and faster closures, it won’t be a choice anymore, it will be a necessity. And that belief is what I bring into this journey: we’re not just improving real estate sales, we’re redefining how it works. Read More
